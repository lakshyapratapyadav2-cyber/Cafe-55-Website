A calm table in a busy city

Café 55 sits inside Hotel Rio Benaras, away from the noise of the main road. Warm lamps, a black and white tiled floor and soft music make it an easy place to sit for hours.

We serve hot and cold coffees, teas, shakes and mocktails, along with pizzas, burgers, kebabs, sizzlers and our clay-pot matka specials. Come for a coffee, stay for dinner.
